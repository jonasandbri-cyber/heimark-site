Support

We're a small team, and a real person answers every email.

Get in touch

  • Email info@heimark.app. We usually reply within one business day.
  • Tell us what you were doing and what happened. A screenshot helps, but please leave out passwords and payment details.

Your account

Can't sign in? Use the same method you signed up with: email, Apple or Google. For email, tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a 6-digit code.

Change your password in the app under Profile → Change password. If you signed up with Apple or Google, you can set one there too. Face ID needs it.

Subscriptions

Heimark has a free plan, plus the paid plans Heimark and Host. They're bought and billed through Apple.

  • Cancel or change your plan: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Heimark. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • Moved to a new phone? Sign in, open Profile and tap "Restore purchases".
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sharing a home

Open a home, tap ••• at the top, then tap + next to the people on that home to invite someone by email. They'll see that home's appliances, tasks, files, notes and contacts, but not your other homes. You can remove them any time from the same place. The Heimark plan shares each home with one person, and Host covers your whole team.

Reminders

If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications for Heimark are on in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Heimark.

Ask Heimark

Ask Heimark answers from what you've saved for a home, so the more you add, the better it gets. You can ask up to about 20 questions a day. After that, Ask Heimark will be ready again a little later.

Delete your account and data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your homes and everything in them, and signs Heimark out of your Apple ID if you used Sign in with Apple. It can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email info@heimark.app from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.
