01Set up

Set up in minutes, your way.

Pick Easy setup and tick what your home has: furnace, water heater, fridge, and anything else you add. Or Walk your home and snap each label as you go. Either way, Heimark builds the maintenance plan for you.

02Ask Heimark

Ask your home anything.

"When did we replace the roof?" "What size filter does the furnace take?" Type it or just say it. Heimark answers from your own records, not the internet.

03Answers you can tap

The answer, and the proof.

Every answer comes with the record it came from: the appliance, the warranty, the receipt or the plumber who did the work. One tap and you're there. Not sure what to ask? Heimark suggests questions about your home.

04It remembers

Tell it once. It remembers.

"The water shutoff is behind the basement stairs." Save notes like that and Heimark uses them when you ask. Tell it "I changed the filter" and the job is logged with today's date.

05Reminders & calendar

It reminds you before it breaks.

Every morning, Today shows what's overdue, what's this week and what Heimark thinks is worth doing next. Snooze it, mark it done, or see the whole year on the calendar.

06Home report

The whole house, in one PDF.

A year in review for your records or your insurer, or a full home history when you sell: every appliance with its age and warranty, every repair, and the papers on file. Share it, save it or print it.

07Share the house

Hand it off. Know it's done.

Share a home with your partner. On Host, bring in the whole team: handymen, co-hosts and managers. Assign a task and they're notified; when it's done, so are you.

Light & dark

Easy on the eyes, day or night.

Heimark follows your iPhone's light or dark setting, or you can pick one in Settings. Same home, same answers, whichever you like.

Asked before

Good questions.

Where do the answers come from?

From your own records: the assets, files, notes and tasks you've saved for that property. Every answer shows where it came from, so you can open the receipt or the manual behind it.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your home records are yours. We don't sell them, show you ads, or use them to train AI. Only the people you share a home with can see it, and you can delete your account and everything in it from the app. Read the privacy policy.

I have nothing organized. Where do I start?

Scan one appliance label. Heimark fills in the details and suggests a maintenance plan, and the house starts to fill itself in from there. Add a photo of a receipt or a manual whenever you come across one.

Can my family or my handyman use it too?

Yes. Heimark lets you share with one person, like your spouse. Host lets you bring in your whole team: handymen, co-hosts and managers. Assign tasks and everyone gets notified when work is assigned and when it's done.

Does it work for more than one property?

Yes. A house, a cabin and a short-term rental each get their own assets, records and tasks, side by side. Heimark covers two; Host covers 3 or 10.

What does it cost?

You can start free with one home and five appliances. Heimark is $39.99 a year (2 weeks free) or $4.99 a month for up to two properties, shared with one person like your spouse. Host is $14.99 a month for 3 homes or $39.99 a month for 10, with your whole team, and yearly plans save more. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
