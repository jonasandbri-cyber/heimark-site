01Ask Heimark

Ask your home anything.

"When did we replace the roof?" "What size filter does the furnace take?" Heimark answers from your own records and shows you the receipt, the manual or the task it came from.

02Ask Heimark · Voice

Or just say it.

Hands full under the sink? Tap the mic and ask out loud. Heimark listens, then answers like you typed it.

03Scan with Heimark

Snap the label. Done.

Point your camera at the sticker on the water heater, furnace or fridge. Heimark reads the brand, model and serial, files the appliance, and suggests a maintenance plan you can edit before you save.

04One place

The whole house, in one place.

Assets, Files & Notes, Contacts and Tasks, for the house, the cabin and the rental. Manuals, receipts, warranties, inspection reports and the plumber's number, right where you'll look for them.

05Reminders & calendar

It reminds you before it breaks.

Set a task once and Heimark keeps the schedule. See what's due today, what's coming up and what's overdue, then snooze it or mark it done. The calendar shows the whole year.

06Share the house

Hand it off. Know it's done.

Invite a partner, a handyman or a property manager. Assign a task and they're notified; when it's done, so are you. Heimark keeps the history, so nobody has to remember who did what.

Asked before

Good questions.

Where do the answers come from?

From your own records: the assets, files, notes and tasks you've saved for that property. Every answer shows where it came from, so you can open the receipt or the manual behind it.

I have nothing organized. Where do I start?

Scan one appliance label. Heimark fills in the details and suggests a maintenance plan, and the house starts to fill itself in from there. Add a photo of a receipt or a manual whenever you come across one.

Can my family or my handyman use it too?

Yes. Heimark lets you share with one person, like your spouse. Host lets you bring in your whole team: handymen, co-hosts and managers. Assign tasks and everyone gets notified when work is assigned and when it's done.

Does it work for more than one property?

Yes. A house, a cabin and a short-term rental each get their own assets, records and tasks, side by side. Heimark covers two; Host covers 3 or 10.

What does it cost?

You can start free with one home and five appliances. Heimark is $39.99 a year (2 weeks free) or $4.99 a month for up to two properties, shared with one person like your spouse. Host is $14.99 a month for 3 homes or $39.99 a month for 10, with your whole team, and yearly plans save more. Cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
