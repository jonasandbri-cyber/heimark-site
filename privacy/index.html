Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what you put into Heimark (your homes, belongings, photos, documents and tasks) so the app can keep track of them for you.
  • We don't sell your information, show you ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Photos you scan and questions you ask Ask Heimark are sent to our AI provider to get you an answer.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time, in the app.

This policy explains how Heimark LLC ("Heimark", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Heimark mobile app and heimark.app (together, the "Service"). If you have questions, email info@heimark.app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a profile photo if you add one. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that service shares with us (Apple lets you hide your real email).
  • Home information: properties (names, addresses, property type and details, cover photos), assets and appliances (brand, model and serial numbers, purchase details), maintenance tasks, reminders, notes, contacts you add (such as a plumber's name and phone number) and the people you invite.
  • Photos and documents: photos of appliances and labels, receipts, manuals, warranties and other files you upload.
  • Ask Heimark: the questions you ask, including questions you speak, and the answers you receive.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support or report a problem.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information: which screens and features you use, and when (for example, that you asked Ask Heimark a question or finished a reminder, never what you typed). It's linked to your Heimark account so we can see how people use the app over time. We use it to understand what works and what to improve. We don't record your screen.
  • Device and diagnostic information: device model, operating system, app version, language, crash reports and performance data.
  • Push notification token: so we can send you reminders, if you allow notifications.
  • Purchase information: your subscription status and history from Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment card.

Device permissions

Heimark only asks for these when you use the feature, and you can turn them off at any time in your device settings:

  • Camera and photos: to scan appliances and labels and to add photos.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to ask Ask Heimark questions by voice. Speech is turned into text using your device's speech recognition service (on iPhone, Apple's), and only the text is sent to Heimark.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the app quickly. Your biometric data never leaves your device, and we never receive it.
  • Notifications: to remind you when maintenance is due.

We do not collect your precise location.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: store your home records, build maintenance plans, send reminders, and share a property with the people you invite.
  • To power Ask Heimark and scanning: to identify appliances in photos, read documents, and answer questions about your home.
  • To manage your account and subscription, including free trials.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and enforce usage limits.
  • To understand how Heimark is used and to fix bugs and improve features.
  • To contact you about your account, security, or changes to the Service. We'll only send you marketing emails if you agree, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

We don't use your photos, documents or questions to train AI models, and our AI provider doesn't use the data we send it to train its models.

3. How information is shared

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

With people you invite

When you share a property, the people you invite can see that property's information (its assets, tasks, files, notes and contacts), as well as your name and profile photo. Your other properties stay private.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Heimark.

If Heimark is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of it. It will remain covered by this policy, and we'll let you know beforehand.

4. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your account, homes, assets, files and photos from our active systems right away. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. We may keep limited records longer when the law requires it (for example, purchase records), or to resolve disputes.

If you only have access to a property someone else shared with you, deleting your account removes your access. The property itself belongs to its owner.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Access and edit: you can see and change your information in the app at any time.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and data. You can also email us to ask.
  • Copy: email us for a copy of your data.
  • Notifications and permissions: you can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in your Apple ID or Google Play settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the UK or the EU), you may have extra rights: to know what we collect, to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms. To use any of these rights, email info@heimark.app. We'll respond within the time the law requires, and we won't treat you differently for asking. If you're in the UK or EU and have concerns, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

6. Security

Your data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited so that only you and the people you share a property with can see it. No service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked. If we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

7. Children

Heimark isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International users

Heimark is run from the United States, and our providers may process data in the US and other countries. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
